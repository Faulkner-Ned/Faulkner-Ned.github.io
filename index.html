About This Site

This is mostly a landing page for my domain where I host some projects and my personal email address. I’m not really a blogger, but I’ll post occasionally when I have something worth sharing.

This site is intentionally minimal, With no trackers, no cookies or JavaScript bloat. The modern web loads megabytes of scripts just to display a few paragraphs of text, alongside tracking your every move. I would rather have a simple, static site that loads quickly and doesn’t track me or anyone else.
